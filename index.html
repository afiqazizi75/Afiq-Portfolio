Capacitive-Sensing Sensor Design Research

Final Year Project on the best sensor design to be used in Capacitive-Sensing method for a smart glove

Conducted research on optimizing capacitive sensor design for a glove-based gaming controller intended for rehabilitation applications. Using shunt-mode capacitive sensing, the study evaluated the impact of sensor size, shape, and placement on motion detection sensitivity. COMSOL Multiphysics simulations were performed to analyze and compare sensor configurations, identifying the designs that provided the highest capacitance variation and most accurate finger movement detection.

  • Research Methodology
  • Simulation
  • Test and Experiment Designs
  • 3D Modeling
  • COMSOL Multiphysics

Outcome

Successfully completed the research by identifying the optimal sensor parameters for maximizing motion detection sensitivity, achieving the project objectives and delivering strong final results. The project strengthened my skills in COMSOL Multiphysics simulation, 3D modeling, experimental design, and data analysis. It also enhanced my research, problem-solving, project management, and stakeholder collaboration abilities through the planning and execution of a complex engineering study within a constrained timeline.
